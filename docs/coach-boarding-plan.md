# Coach-by-coach boarding plan (proposed)

Passengers board in waves of four coaches. Each wave is called on the station PA, shown on the platform TV (Figma page 100, S3) and marked by coach lanes on the platform floor. This avoids one big rush at the doors, gives the guard and compartment assistants time per coach, and makes seat conflicts easier to spot.

Proposed. The train, times and coach count below are an example. Timings need station approval.

## Example train
- **Train:** 7841 সুবর্ণলতা (Subornolota), Dhaka → Sylhet, platform 3. This is a fictional example.
- **Departure:** 16:00. Platform opens at 15:30.
- **Coaches:** 12 (ক–ঠ), in three waves of four. If the train has 8 coaches, use two waves. If it has 16, use four.

## Order and timing
| Time | Step | Coaches | PA | TV (S3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15:30 | Platform opens. Arriving passengers get off first | — | Announcement 0 | "Let passengers get off first" |
| 15:35 | Priority boarding | Anyone needing help, older people, people with infants, any coach | Announcement 1 | Priority banner; all lanes "Wait" except step-free coach ঘ |
| 15:38 | **Wave 1** | ক, খ, গ, ঘ | Announcement 2 | Lanes ক–ঘ "Boarding", the rest "Next" or "Wait" |
| 15:44 | **Wave 2** | ঙ, চ, ছ, জ | Announcement 3 | Lanes ঙ–জ "Boarding", ক–ঘ stay open |
| 15:50 | **Wave 3** | ঝ, ঞ, ট, ঠ | Announcement 4 | Lanes ঝ–ঠ "Boarding" |
| 15:55 | Final call | All coaches | Announcement 5 | All lanes "Boarding · final call" |
| 15:58 | Doors closing | — | Announcement 6 | "Doors closing" |

About 6 minutes per wave, which is roughly 4 coaches × about 70 seats with 2 doors each. Adjust after the pilot from the "time at the gate" measure on S8.

- English: "Doors are closing on Subornolota. Please stand clear of the doors."

Each wave announcement plays twice, one minute apart. The coach letter is printed on the ticket and in the SMS receipt, in Bangla and Latin.

## Rules (proposed)
1. **Late is never refused.** A passenger who missed their wave boards with any later wave or at the final call. Waves organise the crowd; they don't take seats away.
2. **Groups split across coaches** board with their own coach. A child or a person who needs help boards with their companion, using the earlier of the two coaches or priority boarding.
3. **The gate checks everyone, all the time.** The guard (S2) checks the ticket and the person for any passenger. Before a coach's wave, the guard asks the passenger to wait in the concourse rather than refusing entry.
4. **Compartment assistants (S4)** stand at their coaches' doors during the wave, help with seats, and flag conflicts to the TT. They cannot move people to other seats.
5. **No names on public screens or PA.** Calls use train, coach and platform only.
6. **Accessibility:** every call is audio in both languages plus the TV and lane lights. Step-free access is at coach ঘ (D). Staff help on request at any wave.
7. **Delays or a platform change:** announce the change before any wave call. Waves restart with the new time. A platform change always reads both languages twice.

## Equipment and staff
- PA zone for platform 3 with pre-recorded wave messages (TTS or recorded voice), triggered from the station console.
- Platform TV (S3) linked to the same trigger, so the screen and the voice change together.
- Floor markings or hanging signs with the coach letter (ক–ঠ / A–L) where each door stops.
- One compartment assistant per 2–4 coaches. The guard is at the platform entry.

## What the pilot measures
- Average time from first call to doors closed.
- Crowding at doors (staff count per wave).
- Seat conflicts flagged per trip.
- Passengers who missed their wave and boarded later.
- Passenger survey: "Was it clear when to board?"
